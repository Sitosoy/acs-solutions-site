FAQ

Common questions

If your question is not here, email us and we will reply within two business days.

Who are your clients?

We work with small and mid-sized businesses in the United States that sell services or programs through phone or video sales calls, such as consultants, coaches, agencies and professional service firms.

Where is ACS Solutions based?

ACS Solutions LLC is a New Mexico limited liability company with its business address at 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE #10878, Albuquerque, NM 87110. Our services are delivered remotely.

How are services billed?

We invoice in U.S. dollars according to the signed agreement: a fixed monthly fee, a commission on closed sales, or a combination of both. Payment is made by ACH or wire transfer.

Do you sell to consumers?

No. We only provide services to businesses. When we speak with your prospects, we do so on your behalf and following your instructions.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Commitment terms are set in each agreement. Most engagements run month to month after an initial period agreed with the client.
